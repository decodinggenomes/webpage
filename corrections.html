Chapter 6: Phylogenetic trees

  • Page 143, the paragraph following the proof incorrectly claims that (2n-5)!!=(2n-5)(2n-6)!! and uses this as the basis for deriving its asymptotic exponential growth. Instead, consider the following alternative and correct argument:
    1. (2n-5)!! > (2n-6)!! = 2^(n-3)·(n-3)! which we know increases super-exponentially in the limit of large n due to Stirling's approximation.
    2. (2n-5)!! < (2n-4)!! = 2^(n-2)·(n-2)! which also increases super-exponentially.
    3. Therefore (2n-5)!! is bounded above and below by exponentially-growing functions, and must thus itself also grow exponentially in the limit of large n.
